What is a non-fiction book?
AI Overview

A non-fiction book is a book based on real people, actual events, and factual information rather than made-up stories. Authors write them to inform, explain, document, or share real-life experiences.

Common Types

• Biography and memoir: Accounts of a person's life, either written by someone else (biography) or by the person themselves (memoir).
• History: Studies of past events, eras, or civilizations.
• Science and nature: Explanations of how the natural world, space, or technology works.
• Self-help and how-to: Practical guides for personal growth, skills, or everyday tasks.
• Essays and journalism: Collections of commentary, investigations, or real-world reporting.

Non-Fiction vs. Fiction

• Non-fiction aims to be accurate and grounded in fact, research, or lived experience.
• Fiction invents characters, plots, or worlds from the author's imagination.

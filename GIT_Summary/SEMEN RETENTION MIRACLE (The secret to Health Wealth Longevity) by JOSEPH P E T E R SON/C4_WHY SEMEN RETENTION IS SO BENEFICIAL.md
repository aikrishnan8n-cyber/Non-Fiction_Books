# 2. BENEFIT 1 — BOOSTS CONFIDENCE

## 2.1 What does the author claim?

The author argues that semen retention can improve self-confidence and self-esteem.

He connects this benefit to dopamine.

### What is dopamine?

Dopamine is a neurotransmitter involved in several important brain functions.

It participates in:

- Motivation.
- Reward learning.
- Anticipation.
- Goal-directed behaviour.
- Movement.
- Reinforcement of habits.

The author explains that dopamine is involved in reward-motivated behaviour.

When someone anticipates something pleasurable, dopamine-related pathways become active.

## 2.2 The author's explanation

The argument develops as follows:

1. Sexual activity stimulates reward-related brain pathways.
2. Ejaculation is associated with changes in dopamine signalling.
3. Repeated stimulation may create excessive reward-seeking behaviour, according to the author.
4. This may reduce motivation and self-control.
5. Avoiding ejaculation helps regulate dopamine.
6. Greater self-control increases confidence.

He suggests that retaining semen and redirecting sexual energy can improve confidence by changing how the brain responds to reward.



### Practical example

Imagine someone who spends several hours repeatedly pursuing sexual stimulation online.

They feel that the behaviour is interfering with their studies and daily responsibilities.

They decide to change their habits.

They begin spending less time on distracting activities and more time learning, exercising and completing their work.

After a few weeks, they feel more confident.

Why?

They are experiencing greater control over their behaviour and seeing evidence that they can follow through on their decisions.

This is a reasonable behavioural explanation for improved confidence.

### Scientific assessment

The author oversimplifies dopamine.

Sexual activity does involve dopamine signalling, but normal masturbation does not mean that the brain is damaging its dopamine receptors or exhausting its dopamine supply.

Cleveland Clinic explains that dopamine and oxytocin are released during orgasm and that masturbation can have positive effects on mood and stress. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation)

**My conclusion:** Better behavioural self-control may increase confidence. However, the proposed mechanism of semen retention repairing or increasing dopamine levels has not been established.

# 3. BENEFIT 2 — HIGHER ENERGY

## 3.1 What does the author claim?

Peterson argues that semen retention can increase physical energy.

He uses two biological explanations:

1. Vitamin B12 conservation.
2. Increased testosterone.

### Vitamin B12

The author explains that B12 is involved in converting food into usable energy.

It is also important for:

- Red blood cell production.
- Nervous-system function.
- DNA synthesis.
- Cellular metabolism.

He argues that B12 is present in sperm and that ejaculation removes some of this valuable nutrient.

According to his explanation, avoiding ejaculation preserves B12 and therefore helps prevent fatigue.

### Testosterone

The author then makes a more specific claim.

He states that semen retention can increase testosterone by approximately 45% within one week.

He suggests that higher testosterone contributes to:

- Increased energy.
- Improved motivation.
- Better physical performance.
- Improved sexual interest.



## 3.2 Scientific assessment

There are two separate issues.

**First: Vitamin B12.**

Vitamin B12 is essential, but there is no convincing evidence that normal ejaculation causes meaningful B12 deficiency or that avoiding ejaculation provides a significant increase in available B12.

**Second: Testosterone.**

The 45% claim is particularly important to examine.

It comes from a 2003 study involving 28 volunteers. The study reported a testosterone peak on the seventh day of abstinence, reaching 145.7% of the baseline level.

However, that paper was retracted in 2021 because it significantly overlapped with an earlier publication in Chinese. Two of the authors agreed to the retraction. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12659241/?dopt=Abstract)

Therefore, the frequently repeated 45.7% testosterone increase should not be treated as established scientific evidence.

The International Society for Sexual Medicine also states that claims that semen retention increases testosterone and improves general physical and mental health are not supported by adequate scientific evidence. [ISSM](https://www.issm.info/sexual-health-qa/what-is-semen-retention-does-it-have-health-benefits)

### My conclusion

Improving sleep, exercising, eating properly and maintaining healthy habits can help improve energy.

Semen retention should not be considered a proven testosterone-boosting or energy-producing treatment.

# 8. BENEFIT 7 — RELIEVES DEPRESSION

This is an important mental-health claim.

We need to examine it carefully.

## 8.1 What does the author claim?

Peterson suggests that semen retention may improve depression because it increases testosterone.

- Reduced motivation.
- Reduced libido.

## 8.2 Two separate explanations

The author actually combines two different arguments.

### Explanation A — Testosterone and mood

The author believes increasing testosterone could improve emotional well-being.

There is some clinical evidence that testosterone treatment may improve depressive symptoms in certain men with testosterone deficiency.

However, this evidence concerns medically assessed testosterone deficiency and supervised treatment.

It does not demonstrate that semen retention increases testosterone or treats depression.

The Endocrine Society recommends diagnosing testosterone deficiency only when compatible symptoms are accompanied by consistently low testosterone measurements. Testosterone should not be used as a general treatment for depression. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2018-00229)

### Explanation B — Guilt and negative self-image

This is a more psychologically plausible observation.

Consider someone who believes masturbation is morally unacceptable.

After masturbating, they experience guilt.

They repeatedly criticize themselves.

This can contribute to emotional distress.

However, masturbation itself is not medically considered a cause of depression.

Cleveland Clinic notes that guilt can arise from religious, cultural or personal beliefs and that counselling may help people experiencing significant distress. [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/24332-masturbation)

### My conclusion

Semen retention should not be used as a treatment for depression.

If someone experiences persistent sadness, hopelessness, loss of interest or other depressive symptoms, professional mental-health support is more appropriate.

The useful lesson from this section is to understand the difference between a behaviour and the guilt or distress someone associates with it.

# 15. BENEFIT 14 — IMPROVES ATHLETIC PERFORMANCE

## 15.1 What does the author claim?

3. Ejaculation releases reproductive nutrients.
4. Abstinence preserves these resources.
5. The body has more energy available for exercise.
6. Athletic performance improves.

## 15.3 What does scientific research show?

A systematic review and meta-analysis published in *Scientific Reports* in 2022 examined nine crossover studies involving 133 participants, 99% of whom were male.

It found no meaningful overall difference between sexual activity and abstinence in physical fitness measures.

The assessed measures included:

- Aerobic fitness.
- Musculoskeletal endurance.
- Strength.
- Power.

The study examined sexual activity occurring between 30 minutes and 24 hours before physical assessment. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36114261/)

A separate randomized crossover study involving 21 trained male athletes, published in 2025, also found that masturbation-induced orgasm 30 minutes before testing did not impair exercise performance. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41390043/)

### Important qualification

These studies do not prove that every athlete will respond identically.

Individual circumstances matter.

However, they do challenge the general belief that ejaculation automatically weakens athletic performance.

### My conclusion

Training quality, nutrition, hydration, recovery and sleep are far better established determinants of athletic performance than semen retention.

# 23. CHAPTER 4 — 25 IMPORTANT QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

### Q1. What are the 16 benefits mentioned by the author?

They are confidence, energy, self-control, memory, hair growth, deeper voice, depression relief, libido, calculated risk-taking, improved sleep, immune support, attractiveness, root chakra awakening, athletic performance, happiness and more available time.

### Q2. Why does the author connect semen retention with dopamine?

He believes repeated sexual stimulation affects reward pathways and that avoiding ejaculation helps regulate dopamine. The proposed receptor-damage and dopamine-depletion mechanism is not established.

### Q3. What is the author's explanation for increased energy?

He argues that avoiding ejaculation preserves B12 and increases testosterone. Neither meaningful B12 conservation nor sustained testosterone elevation through retention is established.

### Q4. What is the purpose of self-control?

The author believes that controlling sexual impulses develops willpower, which may improve confidence and help people achieve other goals.

### Q5. Why does the author connect memory with phosphorus and zinc?

He believes these nutrients are present in semen and important for brain functioning. He therefore proposes that retention conserves nutrients for the brain. This particular mechanism is unsupported.

### Q6. Does semen retention improve hair growth?

The author claims it does through keratin conservation. However, there is no reliable evidence that masturbation causes hair loss or that semen retention promotes hair growth.

### Q7. Why does the author believe retention deepens the voice?

He connects it with increased testosterone. The claimed testosterone increase is not established, and semen retention is not a proven method of changing adult voice depth.

### Q8. How does the author connect testosterone and depression?

He argues that higher testosterone may improve mood. However, testosterone treatment evidence in men with documented deficiencies does not establish semen retention as a depression treatment.

### Q9. What role does guilt play in the author's explanation?

He suggests repeated guilt following masturbation can damage self-image and create emotional distress. This is a plausible psychological concern, although masturbation itself is not inherently harmful.

### Q10. What is libido?

Libido means sexual desire or sex drive. The author believes abstinence can increase sexual desire and help individuals redirect motivation.

### Q11. What is calculated risk-taking?

It means taking risks after evaluating potential consequences and rewards. The author connects this with testosterone and professional ambition, but does not establish that retention causes better decisions.

### Q12. How does the author connect sleep and testosterone?

He argues that testosterone and sleep influence one another, and that retention may improve sleep by increasing testosterone. The retention connection is unsupported.

### Q13. Why does the author believe semen retention improves immunity?

He argues that semen contains nutrients and antioxidants that support the immune system, so retaining semen conserves these resources. There is no convincing evidence that ejaculation weakens immunity.

### Q14. How does the author connect confidence and attractiveness?

He suggests that improved confidence changes how individuals present themselves, creating a positive social feedback loop.

### Q15. What is the root chakra?

It is the first chakra, traditionally called Muladhara, and associated with foundational energy and grounding. The author connects it with Kundalini and sexual energy.

### Q16. Why does the author discuss Muhammad Ali and Mike Tyson?

He uses them as examples of athletes who allegedly abstained before competitions, supporting the historical belief that sexual restraint improves performance.

### Q17. Does sexual abstinence improve athletic performance?

The available systematic-review evidence does not demonstrate a general performance advantage from abstinence over sexual activity within the studied timeframes.

### Q18. How does the author explain happiness?

He argues that avoiding excessive sexual stimulation helps regulate dopamine and reduces feelings of guilt. The dopamine-depletion explanation is unsupported.

### Q19. What is the most practical benefit in the chapter?

Reducing genuinely excessive or distracting behaviour can free time for learning, exercise, work and relationships.

### Q20. Who is Jenson?

Jenson is a 32-year-old German financial-sector worker whose personal experience is presented as a case study supporting semen retention.

### Q21. What challenge did Jenson undertake?

He attempted 30 days without ejaculation. According to the book, he initially found it difficult but later reported improvements in energy, focus, motivation and confidence.

### Q22. What other changes did Jenson make?

He reportedly stopped smoking marijuana, stopped getting drunk on weekends, improved his fitness and began consuming more inspiring content.

### Q23. What does Jenson identify as the biggest benefit?

He identifies self-mastery: discovering that he could control one powerful personal behaviour and use that experience to improve other areas of life.

### Q24. Why is Jenson's case study not scientific proof?

It is a personal account without objective measurements or controlled comparison. Furthermore, several lifestyle changes occurred simultaneously, so it is impossible to isolate semen retention as the cause.

### Q25. What is the most important message of Chapter 4?

The author wants readers to understand that controlling sexual behaviour can become a method of personal discipline. The most practical lesson is to manage distracting habits and redirect time and attention towards meaningful goals.

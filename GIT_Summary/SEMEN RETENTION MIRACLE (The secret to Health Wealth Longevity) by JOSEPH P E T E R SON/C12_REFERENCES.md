**Title:** Endocrine Response to Masturbation-Induced Orgasm in Healthy Men Following a 3-Week Sexual Abstinence.

## 2.4 Jiang et al. (2003)

**Title:** A Research on the Relationship Between Ejaculation and Serum Testosterone Level in Men.

**Journal:** Journal of Zhejiang University-SCIENCE A.

**Publication year:** 2003.

### What did the research report?

The paper reported a testosterone peak on the seventh day of abstinence.

It gave the frequently repeated figure:

**145.7% of baseline testosterone.**

Many semen-retention websites have used this result to claim that testosterone increases by approximately 45.7% after seven days.

### Very important discovery: The article was retracted

The journal formally retracted the paper in 2021.

The reason was substantial overlap with an earlier Chinese-language publication from 2002.

Two of the authors agreed to the retraction. [Springer Link](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1631/jzus.2003.r236.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### What does this mean for the book?

This is one of the biggest problems with the scientific argument presented by Peterson.

The frequently quoted 45.7% testosterone increase should not be treated as established scientific evidence.

The study remains listed in the book's bibliography, but the bibliography does not mention its subsequent retraction.

**My assessment: Do not use the Jiang et al. paper as reliable evidence that semen retention increases testosterone.**

# 5. IMPORTANT ERRORS AND WEAKNESSES IN THE REFERENCE LIST

The bibliography includes Jiang et al. (2003).

The study was retracted in 2021.

The retraction resulted from significant overlap with an earlier publication.

This matters because Peterson uses the study to support the claim that testosterone increases by 45.7% after seven days.

The reference list needs an updated retraction note.

## 5.2 Incorrect Ayad author information

The book lists:

The published record identifies the authors as:

- Bashir M. Ayad.
- Gerhard Van der Horst.
- Stefan S. Du Plessis.

The reference should preserve the correct surnames and initials. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29043697/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The book lists:

*Biomolecules, 7(4), 42.*

The correct publication is:

*Biomolecules, 7(2), 42.*

This is a bibliographic accuracy issue rather than a problem with the underlying research.

## 6.2 2024 meta-analysis — Abstinence and sperm parameters

**Title:** Effects of Long and Short Ejaculatory Abstinence on Sperm Parameters: A Meta-analysis of Randomized-Controlled Trials.

The review included:

- 13 studies.
- 2,315 participants.

It found that longer abstinence was associated with greater semen volume and sperm concentration, but also greater sperm DNA fragmentation and lower progressive motility.

This reinforces the idea that different semen parameters respond differently to abstinence duration. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38828413/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 6.3 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis

This is an especially useful update because it was published after the book.

**Title:** Effect of Ejaculatory Abstinence Duration on Laboratory Semen Parameters: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Human Studies.

Published: August 6, 2026.

Researchers included:

- 82 studies in the qualitative review.
- 59 studies in the quantitative meta-analysis.

### Principal findings

| Parameter | General association |
|---|---|
| Semen volume | Higher with longer abstinence |
| Total sperm count | Higher with longer abstinence |
| Total sperm motility | Higher with shorter abstinence |
| Progressive sperm motility | Higher with shorter abstinence |
| Sperm concentration | No consistent overall difference |
| Sperm morphology | No consistent overall difference |

The researchers emphasized that evidence certainty was low or very low and that the findings should not be treated as universal clinical thresholds. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42972349/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Why is this valuable?

It provides a more comprehensive modern picture than the selective interpretation in the book.

It demonstrates that the effects of abstinence depend on which reproductive parameter is being measured.
